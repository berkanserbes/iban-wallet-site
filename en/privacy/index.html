Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

IBAN Wallet is built to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not do and how you stay in control. The same text is shown in the app under Settings > About > Privacy Policy.

Data the app stores

The IBANs you save, with their names, descriptions and tags, and your settings (language, theme and app lock). This data is stored only on your device. The developer does not receive or collect any of it.

How your data is protected

IBANs and tags are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is created on your device and kept in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore.

No account, no tracking

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your data over the internet.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes while the scanner is open. Images are not saved or sent.
  • Face ID and fingerprint: handled by your device's operating system. The app only learns whether authentication succeeded.

Sharing and backups

Your data leaves the app only when you copy or share an IBAN, show a QR code or export a backup. Backups are encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you decide; the developer has no access to them. On iOS, app data may also be included in your device's iCloud or computer backup, which Apple encrypts. Android system backup is turned off for this app.

Deleting your data

You can delete any IBAN in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published in the app and on this page with a new date.
