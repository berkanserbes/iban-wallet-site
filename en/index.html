Frequently asked questions

Where are my IBANs stored?

Only on your device, encrypted. IBAN Wallet has no account or server, and your IBANs are never sent over the internet.

Is IBAN Wallet connected to my bank?

No. IBAN Wallet is not a banking app. It does not connect to any bank, move money or show balances. It only helps you keep your IBANs organized and share them.

How do I move my IBANs to a new phone?

Go to Settings > Export Backup, choose a password and save the file (for example to Files, Drive or email). On the new phone, use Settings > Import Backup with the same password. Existing IBANs are kept and duplicates are skipped.

What if I forget my backup password?

The backup cannot be opened without it, and there is no way to reset it. Export a new backup with a password you will remember.

How does the app lock work?

Turn it on in Settings > App Lock. The app then asks for Face ID, fingerprint or your device passcode when it opens and after it has been in the background for the time you choose.

Why can't I take screenshots on Android?

While the app lock is on, screenshots are turned off on Android, so your IBANs do not show in the recent apps screen. Turn the lock off to take screenshots again.

How do I add an IBAN from a QR code?

Open Settings > QR Scanner and point the camera at the code. QR codes shown by IBAN Wallet and codes that contain a plain IBAN are supported.

Why can I only add Turkish IBANs?

The add form currently supports TR IBANs. Support for IBANs from other countries is planned.

I deleted an IBAN by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, tap Undo on the message that appears right after deleting.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete any IBAN in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.
